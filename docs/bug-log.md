# Bug log (QA of the test school)

Found by walking the app as each role on the **test project** (see `docs/qa-test-school.md`). Status: **fixed** (committed, not yet pushed), **open**, or **not a bug** (checked and intended).
Severity: **high** (a feature fails or data could be exposed), **medium** (a feature is slow, confusing or wrong in a common case), **low** (cosmetic or rare).

| # | Where | What happened | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-001 | Integrity dashboard (HOD Analytics > Integrity, school admin) | The page fetched **every exam session with every answer** and filtered on screen. On a test school with 178 sittings it made the database time out (error 57014, 15 to 30 seconds, then a blank page). A real school with thousands of sittings would never load it. | high | **fixed**: asks the database only for flagged essay answers (one query, about 1.2 seconds, flagged answer still shown) |
| B-002 | Every page | Each page asks the sign-in service "who is this?" 8 to 10 times (one call per component), about 1 second each on this connection. Slows every page load, most on phones. | low | open (needs one shared user lookup) |
| B-003 | Smart Learning menu (teachers, heads of department) | **Report Absence**, **My Cover** and **Substitution** opened pages that belong to Smart Assess, so the menu jumped to Smart Assess and its Home item. (Reported by the owner.) | medium | **fixed**: the three pages now open inside Smart Learning (`/learning/report-absence`, `/learning/cover`, `/learning/substitution`) |
| B-004 | Menus in every portal | A page with no menu item of its own (an exam you opened, a student, absence) lit the **Home** item, which looked like being sent back to the home page. (Reported by the owner.) | medium | **fixed**: Home lights only on its own page; pages opened from a section light that section (exam and grading pages light Tests, a student lights My Classes); tested in `scripts/tests/sidebarActive.test.mjs` |
| B-005 | Student sign-in on a page opened over plain http (another laptop on the same Wi-Fi) | Hung on "Signing in..." because the browser feature it used only exists on secure pages. The live site (https) was never affected. | low | **fixed** (`src/lib/randomId.ts`) |
| B-006 | Smart Learning (students) | A student could open staff-only pages by typing the address: the lesson editor, lesson plans, coverage, resources, student support, tutor flags, report absence, my cover, substitution. (The database refused to save anything.) | low | **fixed**: students are sent back to their lessons (checked for all 9 pages) |
| B-007 | Lesson plans (database rule) | The rule said only "the row is yours", so any signed-in account, a student included, could create lesson plans. | medium | **fixed** by migration 094 (`scripts/migrations/094_lesson_plans_staff_only.sql`): teachers, heads of department and the school admin only. 8 database checks. Applied on the test project; **apply on Manchester**. |
| B-008 | Taking an exam (all three exam pages) | An answer clicked in the last 2 seconds before a refresh, crash or closed tab was not kept (answers were saved to the device only after a 2-second pause). | low | **fixed**: saved within 0.3 seconds, and again when the page is hidden or closed. Rechecked with a 1-second reload: all three answers kept. |
| B-009 | Draft exams, exam questions, exam-to-class links (database rules) | **A student, using the app's own database connection (not the normal screens), can create an exam, mark it published, link it to their class, and add questions; another student in that class can then see it.** The rules on `draft_exams`, `questions` and `draft_exam_class_groups` check only "you created it", not that you are staff. Found 2026-10-07 with a probe; probe rows were deleted. The normal student screens do not offer this. | **high** | **open, waiting for your go-ahead.** Proposed fix: add the same staff-role check as migration 094 to those three rules (and review `report_card_comments` and the other "own rows" rules), then re-run the probe. |

## Checked and not a bug
- `cancel_teacher_absence` returns an error on the report-absence and substitution pages: it is a deliberate "is this installed?" check.
- `/principal/ai-tutor` 403 on school settings and `/school-admin/library` 403: both are school switches that are off in the test school.
- Pages that redirect (`/supervisor/classes` to classrooms, `/supervisor/exams` to the teacher's tests, `/teacher/vetting` to the home page for a non-vetting teacher, and similar): intended.
- Phone width: all 124 pages in every role open without sideways scrolling.
