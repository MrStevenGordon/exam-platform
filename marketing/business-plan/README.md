# Business plan

`Smart-Assess-Ja-Business-Plan.docx` — the formal written business plan (Executive Summary through Risks & Milestones), for funders and grant reviewers (e.g. IGNITE).

Financial projections are illustrative and assumption-driven, grounded in the pricing and unit economics on the funders pitch deck. The Year 2–3 operating-expense detail is intentionally left qualitative pending confirmed hiring plans — update it before relying on those figures for planning.
