# Competitor slide: claims checked against public documentation (October 2026)

Background for the "Why not just use Google Classroom?" slide and its speaker notes (the earlier multi-tool comparison table was replaced on 2026-10-08 by the "Good tools. One connected journey." and Google Classroom slides). Each claim below was checked against the vendor's own help pages or documentation where possible. Anything that could not be verified was left off the slide.

## Claims removed from the first draft (could not be supported, or were wrong)
- "By hand or by spreadsheet" for marking: wrong. All four auto-mark objective questions.
- "Separate lockdown tool" for everyone: only Canvas relies on a third-party browser; the others have their own method.
- "HODs and principals rarely see the school", "separate tool for catch-up", "separate system for attendance and timetable": not verified, removed.
- Moodle attendance is an optional plugin, not core (verified at docs.moodle.org/en/Attendance_activity), but it is not on the slide.

## Smart Assess Ja claims on the slide (our own product)
- Lock-down desktop app for Windows and Mac: in the product guide, 6 October 2026.
- Essay marking points; AI-suggested marks a teacher always edits: live on Manchester (migration 082, switch on).
- Missed questions, common wrong answer, who needs support: exam insight (migration 080); results by topic: My Topics (migration 083).
- Smart Play shares the school's topic list; coming soon: product guide.
- Attendance, timetable and cover: school tools in the product guide.
