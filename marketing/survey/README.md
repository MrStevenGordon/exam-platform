# Manchester High School survey (October 2026)

Handwritten paper surveys: 12 students, 10 teachers. Scans are on the external drive (SEC II / HPSCANS: student.pdf, teacher.pdf).
Answers were read by eye from the scans (ticks, circles and slashes) and typed into `data_students.py` / `data_teachers.py`.
Every tick counts; the "select up to N" limits on the forms are not applied (the school's decision).
Faint ticks (teacher 5) and a few ambiguous marks are noted in comments in the data files; counts may be off by one in places.
Written answers are in `free_students.txt` / `free_teachers.txt` (spelling lightly corrected only in the deck and report).
`python3 tally.py` rebuilds `tallies.json`.
